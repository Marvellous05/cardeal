ROYAL MOTORS WEBSITE DEMO
==========================

Files
- index.html: all website sections and sample vehicle listings
- styles.css: responsive design and styling
- script.js: vehicle search/filter/sorting and WhatsApp actions

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the royal_motors_demo folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in your browser.
4. Internet is required for the remote car photos and Google Fonts.

BEFORE SHOWING TO A REAL DEALER
- This is a demo concept, not a real dealership.
- Replace Royal Motors with the prospect's actual business name.
- Replace the sample phone number in index.html and DEMO_WHATSAPP_NUMBER in script.js.
- Replace all sample vehicle listings, prices, specs, location and contact details with verified information.
- Make sure you have permission to use the vehicle photos and business logo.
- Confirm vehicle prices and availability with the dealer.
- The vehicle images are remote demo photos and may not exactly match the listed vehicles.
- No admin dashboard is included. Listings are edited in index.html and the site is redeployed.
- The demo has no online payments or customer accounts.

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript website and can be hosted on a suitable static host.
